Case competition concept

Mehfil

A premium Indian crockery concept that brings together the richness of Indian heritage and contemporary luxury. The idea explored how tableware could become more than functional dinnerware: an expression of culture, craft and the experience of hosting.

Concept collections:

  • Taal: 12-piece collection.
  • Raag: 24-piece collection.
  • Signature: 6-piece premium collectible.

Developed as an idea for a case competition; not presented as a launched business.
